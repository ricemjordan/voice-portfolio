# Voice Audio Engineering Portfolio

**Listen here: https://ricemjordan.github.io/voice-portfolio/**

Before-and-after examples of how I prepare spoken-word audio for ElevenLabs voices and Adobe Speech Enhance, plus a settings study and video dialogue work.

I'm Jordan Rice, an audio engineer and producer in Columbus, OH. I work in Logic Pro, Pro Tools, and Ableton, and I clean voice audio with my own iZotope and Waves chain.

## My voice prep chain

| Step | What I do | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Assess | Check noise floor, room tone, peaks, and consistency across takes | Your meters / RX analysis |
| 2. Repair | De-click, de-plosive, remove isolated noises | iZotope RX |
| 3. Reduce | Light noise reduction and de-reverb, stopping before artifacts appear | iZotope RX |
| 4. Shape | EQ and de-essing to control harshness while keeping natural tone | Waves |
| 5. Level | Gentle compression and loudness matching across files | Waves / UAD |
| 6. Generate | Build the ElevenLabs voice or run Speech Enhance, then review against the source | ElevenLabs, Adobe |

Edit this table to match your exact plugins and settings.
