My voice prep chain

The order matters. Each stage fixes one problem without creating a new one for the next stage, or for the voice model.

  1. 1

    Assess

    Check noise floor, room tone, peaks, and consistency across takes before touching anything.

  2. 2

    Repair

    iZotope RX: de-click, de-plosive, and remove isolated noises.

  3. 3

    Reduce

    Light noise reduction and de-reverb, stopping before artifacts appear.

  4. 4

    Shape

    Waves EQ and de-esser to control harshness while keeping natural tone.

  5. 5

    Level

    Gentle compression and loudness matching so every file sits at the same level.

  6. 6

    Generate

    Build the ElevenLabs voice or run Speech Enhance, then review against the source.

Edit these six steps to match your exact plugins and settings.
